# LeafVox Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last Updated / 最后更新**: July 25, 2026

## English

### Overview

LeafVox is a local-first iPhone app for importing, reading, and listening to supported EPUB and TXT files. This policy explains what information LeafVox handles and how it is protected.

### Data Collection

**LeafVox does not collect personal data through the app.**

LeafVox has no developer-operated account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud service. The app does not upload your books, book text, reading positions, or voice and appearance settings to the developer.

### Data Processed on Your Device

LeafVox processes the following information only to provide app functionality:

- Files you explicitly select with Apple's document picker
- Book content and metadata needed to validate and display the publication, such as title, author, language, cover, format, and file size
- Reading position, import status, and optional per-book voice preference
- App preferences such as default system voice, speech rate, reading theme, and font size

Imported files and derived reading data are stored in the app's private container on your device. LeafVox copies a supported file into that container and does not modify the original file you selected.

### System Services

LeafVox uses Apple's system document picker to let you choose a file. Access is limited to the files you select.

LeafVox uses iOS system speech services for read-aloud playback. System voice availability and any system-managed voice downloads are controlled by Apple and your device settings. LeafVox does not send book text to a developer-operated speech server.

While playback is active, LeafVox may provide the current book title, author, cover, and playback status to iOS so that system Now Playing and lock-screen controls can work.

### Third-Party Software

LeafVox includes open-source components such as Readium, ZIPFoundation, and SwiftSoup for local publication parsing, validation, and display. LeafVox does not use these components for advertising, analytics, tracking, or transmitting your book content to the developer.

### Tracking and Advertising

LeafVox does not:

- Track you across apps or websites
- Use an advertising identifier
- Display third-party advertising
- Use analytics or crash-reporting services that send app data to the developer

### Retention and Deletion

Imported books and their related reading data remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall LeafVox.

To delete a book, open it in LeafVox, tap the **More** button, and choose **Delete This Book**. This removes LeafVox's local copy, cover, reading position, and per-book voice setting. It does not delete or change the original file outside LeafVox.

General app preferences remain until you change them or uninstall the app. Uninstalling LeafVox removes its app-local data from the device. iOS may include app data in device backups according to your Apple backup settings; LeafVox does not operate or have access to those backups.

### Support Communications

If you voluntarily contact support by email, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. This information is used only to respond to your request and is retained only as reasonably necessary for support and legal obligations. Support email is not collected through the LeafVox app.

Please do not send copyrighted books, private documents, or sensitive personal content unless it is necessary for a support request and you have the right to share it.

### Children's Privacy

LeafVox is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Security

LeafVox relies on iOS app sandboxing, device encryption, and your device security settings to protect locally stored data. You are responsible for maintaining access controls on your device and for keeping any original or backup copies of your files.

### Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when LeafVox's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

## 中文

### 概述

LeafVox 是一款本地优先的 iPhone 应用，用于导入、阅读和朗读受支持的 EPUB 与 TXT 文件。本政策说明 LeafVox 会处理哪些信息，以及这些信息如何受到保护。

### 数据收集

**LeafVox 不会通过 App 收集个人数据。**

LeafVox 不提供由开发者运营的账号系统、分析、广告、跨应用追踪或云端服务。App 不会把您的书籍、正文、阅读位置、声音设置或外观设置上传给开发者。

### 在设备本地处理的数据

为提供应用功能，LeafVox 会在您的设备上处理：

- 您通过 Apple 系统文件选择器主动选择的文件
- 验证和展示出版物所需的书籍内容与元数据，例如标题、作者、语言、封面、格式和文件大小
- 阅读位置、导入状态和可选的单本书声音偏好
- 默认系统声音、朗读速度、阅读主题和字号等应用设置

导入的文件和阅读数据保存在设备上的 App 私有容器中。LeafVox 会把受支持的文件复制到该容器，不会修改您所选择的原始文件。

### 系统服务

LeafVox 使用 Apple 系统文件选择器供您选择文件，访问范围仅限您主动选择的文件。

LeafVox 使用 iOS 系统语音服务进行朗读。系统声音是否可用以及系统管理的声音下载由 Apple 和您的设备设置控制。LeafVox 不会把书籍正文发送到开发者运营的语音服务器。

朗读期间，LeafVox 可能会向 iOS 提供当前书名、作者、封面和播放状态，以便系统“正在播放”和锁屏控制正常工作。

### 第三方软件

LeafVox 使用 Readium、ZIPFoundation 和 SwiftSoup 等开源组件在本机解析、验证和展示出版物。LeafVox 不会利用这些组件进行广告、分析、追踪，也不会通过它们把您的书籍内容传输给开发者。

### 追踪与广告

LeafVox 不会：

- 跨 App 或网站追踪您
- 使用广告标识符
- 展示第三方广告
- 使用会向开发者发送应用数据的分析或崩溃报告服务

### 保存与删除

导入的书籍及相关阅读数据会保存在您的设备上，直到您主动删除或卸载 LeafVox。

如需删除书籍，请在 LeafVox 中打开该书，点击右上角的**更多**按钮，然后选择**删除这本书**。该操作会删除 LeafVox 的本地副本、封面、阅读位置和单本书声音设置，不会删除或修改 LeafVox 之外的原始文件。

通用应用设置会保留到您修改设置或卸载 App。卸载 LeafVox 会从设备上移除 App 的本地数据。iOS 可能会根据您的 Apple 备份设置把应用数据纳入设备备份；LeafVox 不运营也无法访问这些备份。

### 技术支持通信

如果您主动通过电子邮件联系技术支持，开发者会收到您选择发送的电子邮件地址、消息和附件。这些信息仅用于回应您的问题，并只在技术支持和法定义务所需的合理期限内保留。LeafVox App 本身不会收集技术支持邮件。

除非排查问题确有必要且您有权分享，否则请勿发送受版权保护的书籍、私人文档或敏感个人内容。

### 儿童隐私

LeafVox 面向一般用户，不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 数据安全

LeafVox 依靠 iOS 应用沙盒、设备加密和您的设备安全设置保护本地数据。您需要负责维护设备访问控制，并自行保管文件的原始副本或备份。

### 政策更新

当 LeafVox 的功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能会更新。页面顶部日期表示当前政策版本。
