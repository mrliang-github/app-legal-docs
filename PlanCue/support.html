Which devices can scan rooms?

Scanning requires a LiDAR-equipped iPhone that supports Apple RoomPlan (iPhone 12 Pro / Pro Max or later supported Pro models) running iOS 17 or later. RoomMark is designed for iPhone. Other compatible iPhones without LiDAR can explore the built-in Sample Plan and open imported .plancue backups, but cannot scan. Buying Pro does not add scanning hardware. Scanning is intended for indoor single-floor spaces (Apple RoomPlan recommends up to roughly 2,000 sq ft / 186 m² per floor).

How do I make a site record?

  1. Start scanning with no account required — RoomMark works offline in basements and new builds. Use Quick Scan (live 2D top-down outline minimap with one-tap Save & Next Room chaining) or switch to Standard 3D anytime from the scanner header menu or Settings. Both modes generate 2D plans and 3D models.
  2. Review the whole home or individual spaces in Floor Plan, 3D Model, and Data on one screen. Auto-straighten, rotate 90°, or fine-tune 2D angles, and non-destructively hide mirror ghost walls or reclassify glass partitions as windows or openings.
  3. Set a uniform ceiling height once for the whole home or per room (with local beam or soffit overrides per wall), and step clockwise (W1, W2…) to enter verified tape or laser meter widths alongside LiDAR estimates.
  4. Attach site photos and notes directly to a wall ID or pin them onto the floor plan so every photo stays tied to the right wall.
  5. Preview and export a US Letter or A4 PDF report or a high-resolution PNG (3000 px) for the whole home or a single space. Recipients do not need RoomMark.

What export formats are supported? Does RoomMark export CAD / DXF?

RoomMark exports clean PDF site reports (whole-home or single space, with optional dimensions, verified wall widths & heights, notes, and photo appendices), high-resolution PNG floor plans (3000 px long edge), and editable .plancue project backups.

RoomMark does not currently export CAD or 3D exchange formats (such as DXF, DWG, SKP, OBJ, CSV, or standalone USDZ files). Internal USDZ files are used for in-app 3D preview only.

Why do scan estimates differ from checked measurements?

LiDAR phone scans are estimates for rapid field layout and communication; accuracy is affected by lighting, obstructions, glass, reflections, and device movement. Always verify critical dimensions with a tape measure or laser meter before construction or fabrication. Manual measurements are entered by hand (external Bluetooth laser meters are not synced automatically) and are stored separately without reshaping the 2D/3D geometry or recalculating area.

What if scanning is slow or walls are missing?

Keep lighting adequate, aim at wall corners or wall-floor junctions from about 1–3 m (3–10 ft), and move steadily without pointing straight down at the floor. Mirrors, glass, clutter, and blank walls can slow tracking. After saving, you can non-destructively hide mirror ghost walls, reclassify glass walls as windows or openings, or rescan a room and compare before replacing.

What if saving or exporting fails?

Keep the current screen open, retry as prompted, and check available device storage. If the issue continues, email support with the exact message and steps. Avoid uninstalling while troubleshooting until you have exported editable .plancue backups of important jobs. PDF and PNG files are for viewing and sharing and cannot restore an editable project.

Free access, Pro plans, and restoring purchases

No account or payment method is required to try RoomMark. You can explore the built-in Sample Plan right away and scan 1 real project free (up to 2 saved rooms, including 2 free rescans). Saved projects stay viewable on your iPhone, and you can organize, archive, export .plancue backups, and import copies anytime. Export previews show a watermarked first-page draft until Pro is unlocked.

RoomMark Pro unlocks unlimited projects, multi-room whole-home scanning (3+ rooms), editing beyond the free project, and clean watermark-free PDF reports and high-resolution PNG exports. Choose Yearly (renews annually; cancel anytime in Apple subscription settings) or Lifetime (one-time payment, no recurring charges). Current prices and terms are shown on the App Store purchase screen. Users who previously claimed the legacy 7-day trial keep access until that 7-day period expires; it never auto-charges.

To restore on a new device or after reinstalling, sign in with the Apple Account used for purchase and tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings. Request refunds through Apple's Report a Problem service.

How do I organize and back up a project?

Open Projects to search, organize with folders and project types, or archive completed jobs (archived projects can be restored anytime). Export an editable .plancue project backup to Files before deleting important records or uninstalling the app. Backups preserve room models, photos, verified measurements, notes, and saved report versions (up to 128 MiB per file). Importing creates a new copy and never overwrites existing projects.

Can I change languages, units, or paper size?

In Settings and Help, switch between English, Simplified Chinese, or Follow System (other system languages default to English), and choose feet & inches (with fractional inches and sq ft), meters, or millimeters. In Export and Share, choose US Letter or A4, toggle individual report sections (dimensions, verified measurements, notes, photos, project info), and save static report versions that remain unchanged if you edit the project later.

Are my records uploaded?

No. Scans, floor plans, dimensions, photos, and notes stay on your iPhone. RoomMark does not upload your projects to the developer or third-party AI services. Device system backups may include local files depending on your iOS settings, and files you explicitly share are passed only to the apps or recipients you choose. See the Privacy Policy.
