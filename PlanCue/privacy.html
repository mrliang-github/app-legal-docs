Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 17, 2026

PlanCue is an iPhone tool for keeping room and site measurement records. Scans, plans, dimensions, notes and photos stay on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer or third-party AI services.

1. Data on your device

The app processes and stores room scans, floor plans, 3D models, scan estimates, measurements you enter manually, project names, notes and selected photos locally. It also stores unit preferences, trial status and caches for model previews and file exports on your device.

No app account is required. The app contains no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs and does not use your records for tracking. The developer cannot remotely access projects saved in the app.

2. Camera and photos

The app requests camera permission when you scan a room or take a site photo. When you choose a photo, the app saves a local copy for the project without modifying the original in your photo library. Photo import uses the system picker and reads only the items you select.

You can manage camera and other system permissions in iOS Settings. Room scanning and taking photos are unavailable when camera access is disabled.

3. Purchases and trial access

Apple handles purchases, payment, renewals and refunds. The app uses StoreKit product information and verified transaction status to provide access and restore purchases. It does not receive payment card details.

The 7-day trial start date and status prevent the trial from restarting. The trial never automatically charges you when it ends. A yearly subscription renews annually only after you choose to purchase it. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal. Apple's privacy policy applies to its handling of account and transaction data.

4. Exporting and sharing

Only when you choose to share a file does the app pass the selected PDF or image to the iOS share service and your chosen app or recipient. Exported files may contain plans, dimensions, names, notes and photos, depending on your selections. Recipients handle files under their own policies.

5. Storage, backups and deletion

You can delete projects in the app. iOS system backups may include local app data, depending on your settings. Uninstalling the app may cause local project data to be lost. Deleting a project does not automatically recall files that you have shared or saved elsewhere.

PDF and PNG exports are for viewing and sharing; they cannot restore an editable project. Keep copies of any records you need before deleting projects or uninstalling.

6. Contacting support

7. This website and policy updates

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process web requests according to its Privacy Statement. This website adds no advertising or analytics scripts.

If the product's data handling changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date. Please contact the email address above with questions about this policy.
