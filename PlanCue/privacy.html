Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: November 25, 2026

RoomMark is an iPhone tool for keeping room and site measurement records. Scans, plans, dimensions, notes, and photos stay on your device. The app does not upload them to the developer or third-party AI services.

1. Data on your device

The app processes and stores room scans, floor plans, 3D models, scan estimates, measurements you enter manually, project names, folders, notes, selected photos, site records, and saved report versions locally. It also stores language and unit preferences, local free-quota and entitlement cache states, and caches for model previews and file exports on your device.

No app account is required. The app does not show ads, track your activity across other apps or websites, or send app-usage analytics to the developer. The developer cannot remotely access projects saved in the app.

2. Camera and photos

The app requests camera permission when you scan a room or take a site photo. When you choose a photo, the app saves a local copy for the project (stripping unnecessary location metadata) without modifying the original in your photo library. Photo import uses the system picker and reads only the items you select.

You can manage camera and other system permissions in iOS Settings. Room scanning and taking photos are unavailable when camera access is disabled.

3. Purchases and free access

Apple handles purchases, payments, renewals, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit product information and verified transaction status to provide access and restore purchases. It does not receive payment card details.

Your device Keychain stores only local free-quota usage (free project, room, and rescan limits), verified Pro entitlement cache for offline recognition, and timing state for legacy trials or one-time offers. Free access never automatically charges you. A yearly subscription renews annually only after you choose to subscribe; Lifetime access is a one-time payment with no automatic renewal. Apple's privacy policy applies to its handling of account and transaction data.

4. Exporting and sharing

Only when you choose to share a file does the app pass the selected PDF, image, or editable .plancue project backup to the iOS share service and your chosen app or recipient. Exported files may contain plans, dimensions, names, notes, photos, site records, and saved reports, depending on the file type and your selections. An editable project backup contains the project's records and files. Recipients handle files under their own policies.

5. Storage, backups, and deletion

You can delete projects in the app. iOS system backups may include local app data, depending on your settings. Uninstalling the app may cause local project data to be lost. Deleting a project does not automatically recall files that you have shared or saved elsewhere.

PDF and PNG exports are for viewing and sharing; they cannot restore an editable project. Export an editable .plancue project backup to a location you control before deleting important projects or uninstalling. Importing a backup creates a new project copy.
