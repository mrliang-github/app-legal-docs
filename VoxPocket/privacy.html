Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 25, 2026

VoxPocket records audio on Apple Watch or iPhone and transcribes it on your iPhone. Recordings and text stay on your devices. The app does not upload them to the developer or a third-party transcription service.

Content on your device

The app stores recordings, titles, original and edited transcripts, timestamps, language preferences and free transcription usage locally. It has no app account, ads, cross-app tracking or developer analytics.

Microphone and transcription

The app uses your microphone when you choose to start a recording. You can manage microphone permission in system Settings. Turning it off prevents new recordings; existing notes remain available.

Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech features. Preparing a language for the first time requires an internet connection; Apple downloads and manages those resources. Transcription runs on a compatible iPhone, with no cloud transcription fallback.

Between Watch and iPhone

Watch recordings are transferred to your paired iPhone using Apple's paired-device communication features. Transcripts and related status can be sent back to Watch. The system may delay transfers or processing. VoxPocket does not use a developer server or provide in-app cloud sync.

Purchases and the free allowance

Your first five completed transcriptions are free. Failed attempts do not count, and retrying the same recording does not use another allowance. Usage is stored locally. Apple handles purchases, payments, restoration and refunds. The app checks prices and purchase entitlements through Apple's purchase services; it receives no payment card information and sends no recordings or transcripts to verify a purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. Apple's privacy policy applies to its account and transaction processing.

Sharing, deletion and backups

When you choose to share, the selected recording or text is passed to the system share service and the receiving app you select. The recipient's policies apply. Deleting a local recording does not recall copies already shared.

Recordings, text, settings and temporary files are excluded from system backups. Deleting the app or a recording, or losing your device, may cause permanent data loss. Export important content to a location you choose. VoxPocket has no cloud recovery service. Deleting recordings does not reset the free allowance.

Contacting support

This website and updates

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process network requests under its privacy statement. We add no advertising or analytics scripts. Changes to the app's data handling will be reflected in this policy with an updated date.
