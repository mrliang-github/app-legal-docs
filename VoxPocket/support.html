Record now. Read it back later.

Capture an idea or meeting on Apple Watch or iPhone, then read, edit and replay it on your iPhone.

Your first recording

  1. Open Settings (设置) in VoxPocket on iPhone. Choose Mandarin or English and download its language resources. You can record before the download is ready.
  2. Tap the microphone on iPhone or open VoxPocket on your paired Apple Watch. Allow microphone access when asked.
  3. Stop the recording to save it. Watch recordings are transferred to iPhone for on-device transcription.
  4. Open a note to read, edit or copy the text. Tap a timestamp to replay a passage, or use the share menu to export text or audio.

Devices and languages

Requires iOS 26 or later. Watch features require watchOS 26 or later and a paired iPhone. Transcription also requires a device and language supported by Apple's on-device speech features; availability is shown in the app. The interface is currently in Simplified Chinese. Mandarin and English transcription are supported. Pro does not add transcription support to an incompatible device.

Waiting for a recording or transcript?

Check that Watch and iPhone are paired and connected, then open VoxPocket on both. Download the chosen language on iPhone before transcribing. The system may delay transfer or processing; reopen the iPhone app if a note is waiting. “已存手表” means the recording is saved on Watch, not yet confirmed on iPhone. Keep the original until reception is confirmed.

Five free transcriptions and Pro

The first five completed transcriptions are free. Failed attempts do not count, and retrying the same recording does not count twice. Pro unlocks unlimited transcriptions with one purchase, no subscription or automatic renewal. After using the free allowance, you can still record, play and share audio, and view, edit and export existing text. Prices appear in the App Store.

To restore a purchase, open Settings (设置) and select Restore Purchases (恢复购买), using the original Apple account and an internet connection. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Export important content

VoxPocket has no in-app cloud sync. Recordings, text and settings are excluded from system backups. Data may not be recoverable after deleting the app or losing your device. Export TXT, Markdown or original audio before making changes. Shared files are handled by the receiving app. Speech recognition can make mistakes; replay important details to check them.
